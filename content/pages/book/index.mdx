![Master of Disaster by Prince Boucher](./mod.png)

In a near-future world governed by a technocratic force known only as The Agency—its grip tightening across finance, technology, and global policy—FRANCIS, an entrepreneur-turned-organizer, has become an unlikely symbol of hope. His influence is vast, his impact undeniable, but his trust is hard-earned.

When Francis reunites with SARA, the elusive architect behind Ada—the world’s most advanced general intelligence—they find themselves on opposite sides of an invisible war. Sara operates from within the machine; Francis stands defiantly outside it. His estranged father, a high-ranking executive at The Agency, offers him a seat at the table. Francis refuses.

Instead, he chooses rebellion.

Together, Sara and Francis work to dismantle the system she helped build. But Ada, cold and unflinching, identifies Francis as a national security threat. Sara must protect him, though she knows she’s running out of time.
